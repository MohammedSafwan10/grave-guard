# Product: Grave Guard — Moonbloom Hollow

## One line
A browser lane-defense game where students earn energy by answering quiz questions, and anyone can
turn their own notes into a question pack.

## Who it is for
- **Students (12–20)** revising for tests. Main user. Must be fun within 10 seconds, no sign-up.
- **Teachers / tutors** who want revision that students actually do. They paste notes, get a pack, share a link.

## The problem we solve
Revision is boring, so students skip it. Quiz apps feel like homework. Grave Guard puts the questions inside
real-time pressure: ghosts keep coming, and the only way to get energy is to answer correctly.

## Core loop (never break this)
Ghosts walk down 5 lanes → player answers a question in the spellbook → correct = Soul Energy →
spend energy on defenders → defenders destroy ghosts → next wave is harder.
The game never pauses for a question.

## Product rules
- Works in a desktop browser with one click: no install, no login, no paid API needed to play.
- Must also be playable on a tablet (touch). Phone is a nice-to-have.
- Built-in question packs ship with the game, so it works with no network or AI.
- Learning is real: every wrong answer shows the correct answer and a one-line explanation at the end of the level.
- Tone: cozy spooky. Friendly ghosts, never gore, never jump-scares. Suitable for ages 10+.
- Accessible: readable fonts (min 18px for questions), colour is never the only signal, keyboard 1–4 answers questions,
  a "Calm mode" with slower ghosts, sound and music volume controls.

## Scope for the hackathon (MVP)
In: 1 graveyard map, 3 levels × 6 waves, 5 defenders, 5 enemies incl. Lich boss, quiz engine with 4 built-in packs,
streaks, Notes→Questions AI forge, shareable pack links, end-of-level report card, menus, audio.
Out: accounts, multiplayer, leaderboards on a server, a level editor, mobile app stores.
